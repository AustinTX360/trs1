    About

    A local supplier for Texas labs

    Texas Research Supplies is an Austin-based supplier of laboratory chemicals, equipment, and materials for research teams across Texas.

    We keep ordering simple: tell us what you need, and we'll come back with a clear quote. If a product isn't in our catalog, ask anyway, because we may be able to source it.

    Ordering information

    • Pricing is provided by quote and depends on quantity and shipping requirements.
    • Some products are regulated hazardous materials. These are sold only to research institutions and businesses, and they ship in accordance with applicable regulations.
    • Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are available on request.
    • Payment is by wire transfer, ACH, or institutional purchase order. Payment details are sent with your quote.
